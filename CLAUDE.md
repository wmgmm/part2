## What this is

The AI in the Workplace Part 2 workshop website for Cardiff University staff: six guided exercises (called Exercises in the UI; still `MISSIONS` in code) plus a "Useful Prompts" library strip, anchored on ONE real document, Cardiff's Sustainable Futures plan 2025-35. Teaches Gemini Deep Research, Gemini Notebook, Gemini Canvas and Copilot.

**The workshop is split between two presenters, and this constrains editing.** Exercises 01-03 are Matt Hayden's: his prompts are held as `MH_*` constants at the top of `missions.js` and are VERBATIM from his deck (`AI in the workplace 2.pptx`, slides 7-24) and his covering email. The room follows his deck while working through the site, so any divergence shows up live. Do not reword, retitle, reformat or "improve" them. Exercises 04-06 are Matt Mort's and can be edited freely, to the prompt standard below.

Exercise 02 works from a picture on the page (`public/cardiff_study_space.webp`) via the optional step `image` field. **04-06 hand out three working skills rather than asking anyone to write one** (redesigned 2026-09-04). The brief is to build staff training on the plan. 04 The Module runs `Training_Module_Builder.md`; 05 The House Style composes `Cardiff_House_Style.md` on top and then generates the deck; 06 The Check runs `Fact_Check_Cardiff.md` on what they built, then lands the day on the workflow record and the Jisc ladder. The skills are **downloaded, not copied**: that is the lesson. They are generated by `tools/make_artifacts.py`, follow the anatomy in `docs/research/2026-09-04-skill-authoring-patterns.md`, and must each stay under 6,000 characters so they paste into Copilot Agent Builder. **Studio generations are the binding constraint** and Google publishes no number for infographics, decks or data tables; the design budgets one of each per participant, so do not add a step that regenerates. See `docs/research/2026-09-04-tool-capabilities.md`. Exercise 06 is the skill-file exercise: a prompt packaged as a plain text file you attach, deliberately NOT Gems, which Cardiff staff accounts cannot create (re-confirmed 2026-09-04, and it contradicts Google's published Education availability). Copilot Agent Builder and Gemini Skills in Chrome appear only as gated stretch paths.

**Page order:** eyebrow, title and tool cards, then the `brief` (the goal, in black), then `toolInfo.feature` (what the tool is, in grey). Goal before explanation: the brief is what they came for. Set in `MissionDetail.jsx` and applies to all six.

**House copy standard (set 2026-09-04, do not re-inflate).** Exercise pages follow The Matts' Part 1 page at https://wmgmm.github.io/thematts/workshop.html: a `brief` of 25-40 words stating the goal with no narrative, step `title`s as imperative actions, and step `body`s of one or two sentences capped at about 45 words, naming exact controls ("the Tools menu under the box where you type"). Site total is about 1,100 words of step body; it was 3,000 before the cut. Each exercise's completion `verdict` is a mini learning point, not a punchline: the one transferable lesson from that exercise, attributed to The Matts via `verdictBy`. Chris Gravitas now survives only in the gallery memo and the prompt library. Verified tool limits live in `docs/research/2026-09-04-tool-capabilities.md`; read it before writing a step that promises a feature. The pivot roadmap is `ROADMAP.md`; the build log lives in `tasks/todo.md`.

## Architecture

Single-page React app (Vite, framer-motion). No router library. `App.jsx` renders SPLASH (login) until a user exists, then the mission gallery or a mission page selected by hash routing (`#/m1` … `#/m6` plus `#/prompts` for the library, via a `hashchange` listener that ignores plain anchors like `#main-content`; unknown routes fall back to the gallery). The library lives in the `PROMPT_LIBRARY` export in `missions.js`, outside the `MISSIONS` array. URL mode: `?doctor` (facilitator preflight, `DoctorPanel`).

**All prompts follow `docs/PROMPT_GUIDANCE_2026.md`** (the expert prompt-engineering standard: RTF structure, answer shaping, failure states, verbatim grounding). Consult it before editing any prompt.

**Content lives in `src/data/missions.js`** -- briefs, steps, prompts, artifacts, Gravitas verdict lines. Components are generic renderers; to change an exercise, edit the data file. Step fields: `tier: 'core' | 'stretch'` (renders under "Today's path" vs "Take home"), `estMinutes`, `check: true` (the "30-second check" ritual), `prompt` (copyable PromptBox), `promptNote` (a line rendered under the prompt but deliberately NOT copied by the COPY button or the select-text fallback, because it sits outside the `<pre>`; use it for "[attach the ...]", which is an instruction to the reader rather than text to send), `image: {src, alt, caption}` (a picture the step works from; `src` is relative to the site root), `type: 'sort'` (SortGame), `laneNotes` (per-Copilot-licence guidance keyed by lane id).

**Key components** (`src/components/`): `SplashScreen` (name + email), `MissionGallery`/`MissionCard` (card grid; whole card is a button opening the exercise), `MissionDetail` (brief, artifact download strip, tiered steps, check-in, verdict), `PromptBox` (copy with select-text fallback), `SortGame` (green/amber/red data-classification sort).

**Persistence** (`src/lib/progress.js`): localStorage. `workshop_user_v1` holds `{name, email, lane}` for refresh-resume; `workshop_progress_v1:<lowercased email>` holds per-exercise completion. `?doctor` HEAD-checks every artifact, step image and logo, derived from `MISSIONS` so it cannot drift. The leaderboard UI was deleted on 2026-09-04; the backend half is kept for the planned check-in wall: `src/lib/leaderboard.js` (only `recordAttendance` is called, on sign-in), `src/config.js` and the Apps Script mirror `google_apps_script.js`.

**Legacy escaperoom components were deleted** (Countdown, SubmissionPortal, Success/Failure screens, HintReveal, CopilotHint, EvidenceGallery/Card, data/gravitas.js, data/hints.js, assets/placeholders SVGs). `Leaderboard`, `LeaderboardPage` and `AdminPanel` went the same way on 2026-09-04, with their CSS and the `?admin` / `?leaderboard` URL modes. Still live: `TaglineBar`, `SortGame` (the `type: 'sort'` step renderer, currently unused by content).

**Styling**: single file `src/styles.css`. Palette matched to the thematts google.html workshop page (page `#f5f5f7`, white cards, charcoal `#1d1d1f`, blue accent `#0071e3`) over the original tabloid/evidence-tag devices (stamps, tags, Playfair Display). CSS custom properties at `:root`; later "FIELD MISSIONS" and "GOOGLE-PAGE SKIN" sections override earlier rules by cascade order -- append restyles there rather than editing old blocks. Exercise grid auto-flows (not hard-wired to a card count).

**Artifacts** are served from `public/placeholders/` and downloaded via a synthetic `<a download>` (same-origin only). A step can also carry its own card via the step field `artifact`, for a file used only at that point rather than for the whole exercise (Exercise 05 puts the house style skill inside the pencil step). `DoctorPanel` collects step artifacts as well as `mission.artifacts`, so moving a file into a step does not drop it from the preflight. Text artifacts (`.md`, `.txt`) get three buttons of equal width: DOWNLOAD, VIEW IN BROWSER and a **COPY** button. VIEW opens a `text/plain` blob rather than linking the file, because servers send `.md` as `text/markdown` and browsers download that instead of rendering it. COPY exists that fetches the file and writes its contents to the clipboard, because a skill has to go two ways: attached as a file in Copilot or Gemini, or pasted into Gemini Notebook's Studio pencil, which takes text and not files. PDFs keep VIEW IN BROWSER instead; a `.md` link would only download again. Five files. Two are real and not generated: `Sustainable-Futures-en.pdf` (the published plan, 10.7 MB) and `Deep_research_output.pdf` (a real Deep Research run of Matt Hayden's Exercise 01 brief, and the input for the Copilot step when someone's monthly quota is spent). Three are the skill files generated by `python3 tools/make_artifacts.py`: `Training_Module_Builder.md`, `Cardiff_House_Style.md` and `Fact_Check_Cardiff.md`. `Document_QA_Skill.txt` was retired on 2026-09-04. `AI_Position_Statement.pdf` and `Policy_Landscape_Report.pdf` were deleted on 2026-09-04 with the move to a single anchor document, the old `Venture_*` files with the pivot before it, and the escaperoom `evidence_*`/`placeholder_*` images earlier the same day (git history has them all).
