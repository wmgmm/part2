Complaint themes from one term of customer feedback (ranked)
============================================================

1. Long queues at lunchtime and selling out by 1pm (48% of comments;
   two faces of the same capacity problem)
2. Price and value: fine for a treat, too dear for daily (20%)
3. Dietary and allergens: more gluten-free, vegan option, visible labels (13%)
4. Payment friction: card machine reliability, no contactless day (8%)
5. Location and hours: open earlier, trade Fridays, better signage (6%)
